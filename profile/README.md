## 📌 What is xScouting?

**xScouting** is an interactive, web-based platform designed for building tactics, planning squad depth, and analyzing team structures without unnecessary bloat. Whether you are crafting radical offensive systems, testing pressing traps for *Football Manager*, or breaking down real-world match strategies, xScouting gives you a clean canvas to bring your tactical vision to life.

---

## ⚡ Driven by Pure Football

We believe in positional fluidity, high intensity, intelligent ball management, and aesthetic football. Traditional board tools were either outdated, hidden behind paywalls, or slow. 

xScouting was created to be:
* **Fast & Client-Side First**
* **Focused Entirely on User Experience**
* **No Mandatory Account Creation & No Intrusive Tracking** — *just tactics.*
